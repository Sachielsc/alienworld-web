### Raised by the orchestrator, not by a seat

1. **`ask.mjs` preflight ignored `--timeout`** (fixed - see Seat status above).
2. **The designer's critique was silently truncated** at 2629 chars. `finish_reason` came back
   `"length"` - `maxTokens: 4000` was exhausted by a model that reasons before answering - and
   `ask.mjs` reported a cheerful `OK - 2629 chars`. Findings 4 through 8 were simply lost, and
   a critique cut off mid-finding reads exactly like a complete one. Fixed.
3. **A phantom the orchestrator caught on itself.** An early measurement showed 61px of
   horizontal overflow at 390px, apparently from the nav bar. It did not reproduce on a fresh
   load: it was a stale-layout artifact of resizing the viewport without re-navigating. **There
   is no overflow bug.** `baseline-390.png` is contaminated by that artifact (it is 451px wide);
   `final-390.png` is the trustworthy capture. Lesson for the skill: always re-navigate after
   `browser_resize` before measuring or capturing.

## What actually changed


**Phase B (the build):**

- `theme.css` - one new scoped block. `.aw-movie dt` becomes Inconsolata, uppercase, 0.8rem,
  `rgba(151,255,119,0.75)`; `.aw-movie dd` gets 10px left padding, a 1px left rule and
  `overflow-wrap: anywhere`. The two `.white-frame` lead-in cards get a 3px `#97ff77` left rule
  and 2.5x `--aw-card-gap` top margin, with the first card excluded via `:not(:first-child)`.
  Before the change `dt` and `dd` computed identically (16px, `#98a6ad`, `margin-left` zeroed by
  Bootstrap), which is what made the metadata unscannable.
- `movies.html` - all 8 Plot rows became real links. Two of them (`ALIEN³`,
  `Alien: Resurrection`) previously held a `...` placeholder.

**Phase D (applied on Charles' instruction - R7 only):**

- `movies.html` - `aria-label="<film> on Wikipedia"` on all 8 Plot links.

**Tooling, outside the page scope:**

- `ask.mjs` - preflight honours an explicit `--timeout`; truncated responses are now detected
  via `finish_reason` and reported as `PARTIAL` with a loud warning.
- `config.json` - designer `maxTokens` raised 4000 -> 12000.
